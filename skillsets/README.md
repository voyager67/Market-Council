# Trader Skillsets

Ten trader-focused bundles organise the 74-skill library by strategy, asset class, and professional role. Each manifest distinguishes required skills from recommended or optional expansions and lists its related workflow blueprints. Skillsets are research/tooling bundles, not broker configurations, model guarantees, or investment recommendations.

- [`equity-active-trading`](./equity-active-trading.json) — active equity research
- [`growth-and-momentum`](./growth-and-momentum.json) — growth and momentum research
- [`dividend-income`](./dividend-income.json) — dividend quality and income research
- [`macro-fx-commodities`](./macro-fx-commodities.json) — macro, FX and commodities
- [`options-volatility`](./options-volatility.json) — options and volatility research
- [`futures-trading`](./futures-trading.json) — futures and derivatives research
- [`digital-assets`](./digital-assets.json) — digital asset market structure
- [`quant-systematic`](./quant-systematic.json) — quantitative and systematic research
- [`portfolio-and-risk`](./portfolio-and-risk.json) — portfolio and risk management
- [`trader-development`](./trader-development.json) — trader process and improvement

The repository validator confirms that required, recommended and optional skills resolve and that the ten bundles collectively cover the entire skill catalogue.
