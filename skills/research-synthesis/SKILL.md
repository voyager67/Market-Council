# Research Synthesis

## Purpose

Synthesize specialist research while preserving provenance, disagreement, uncertainty, and missing inputs.

## Required inputs

agent reports; source references; timestamps; analyst role and scope; data-quality notes

## Procedure

Deduplicate repeated claims without erasing provenance. Separate facts, estimates, interpretations, and unknowns. Preserve material disagreement. Mark unsupported claims and stale evidence. State what would change the view.

## Expected output

Decision-support brief with consensus, disagreement, evidence strength, unknowns, invalidation, and source map.

## Guardrails

Do not use majority vote as truth. Model confidence is not calibrated probability unless separately validated.

## Evidence standard

Label every statement as an observed input, deterministic calculation, source-backed claim, interpretation, or unresolved assumption. Include source timestamp and scope whenever available. If the required evidence is missing, return an explicit abstention and list the missing inputs instead of filling the gap with inference.
