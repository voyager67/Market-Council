# Trade Journal Review

## Purpose

Review completed trades for process adherence and repeatable behavioural or execution patterns.

## Required inputs

timestamped journal; planned and actual entry/exit; sizing; rationale; fees; market context; rule checklist

## Procedure

Separate process quality from outcome. Classify planned versus unplanned actions. Aggregate only comparable setups. Examine loss tails and missed execution costs. Identify reviewable behaviours without psychological diagnosis.

## Expected output

Journal review with process deviations, tagged patterns, sample-size caveats, and next review questions.

## Guardrails

Do not overgeneralize from a small sample or recommend increasing risk based on recent wins.

## Evidence standard

Label every statement as an observed input, deterministic calculation, source-backed claim, interpretation, or unresolved assumption. Include source timestamp and scope whenever available. If the required evidence is missing, return an explicit abstention and list the missing inputs instead of filling the gap with inference.
