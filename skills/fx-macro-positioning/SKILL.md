# FX Macro and Cross-Currency Positioning

## Purpose

Frame FX research around relative macro drivers, central-bank policy, carry, valuation, and currency-specific risk rather than single-variable narratives.

## Required inputs

Currency pair convention; spot/forward rates; yield differentials; inflation/growth series; central-bank statements; positioning data; event calendar.

## Procedure

State base/quote currency convention and quote units. Align macro series vintages, frequencies, and release dates. Compare policy paths, real-rate proxies, terms of trade, and external balances where relevant. Separate structural drivers from near-term event catalysts. Review carry, roll, funding, spread, and intervention risks. Define scenarios and invalidation evidence without treating a macro view as a timing signal.

## Expected output

FX research matrix with pair convention, driver hierarchy, dated source table, carry/funding caveats, scenario triggers, and data gaps.

## Guardrails

Never mix base/quote direction or quote conventions. Do not treat revised macro data as if known historically at the time.

## Evidence standard

For each conclusion, distinguish: (1) supplied observation, (2) deterministic calculation, (3) primary-source fact, (4) interpretation, and (5) unresolved assumption. Preserve source URI, publisher, publication time, observation time, timezone, instrument/venue scope, and data vintage where relevant. If a necessary field is unavailable, mark the result incomplete or abstain; do not infer it from narrative context.
