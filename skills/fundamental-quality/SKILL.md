# Fundamental Quality

## Purpose

Assess issuer operating quality from supplied primary-source financial statements and disclosures.

## Required inputs

issuer filings; financial statements; reporting currency; fiscal period; accounting basis

## Procedure

Verify document period and units. Review revenue, margins, cash conversion, leverage, dilution, and business concentration. Separate reported facts from interpretation. Identify accounting and cyclicality caveats.

## Expected output

Quality assessment with cited source identifiers, period comparisons, strengths, vulnerabilities, and unresolved questions.

## Guardrails

Do not fabricate filings or normalize accounting items without explanation. Do not call a business high-quality from ratios alone.

## Evidence standard

Label every statement as an observed input, deterministic calculation, source-backed claim, interpretation, or unresolved assumption. Include source timestamp and scope whenever available. If the required evidence is missing, return an explicit abstention and list the missing inputs instead of filling the gap with inference.
