# Dividend Sustainability Review

## Purpose

Assess dividend coverage, cash generation, leverage, capital needs, payout policy, and the risk that distributions are unsustainable.

## Required inputs

Cash-flow and income statements; balance sheet; share count; dividend history; debt maturities; sector context; reporting periods.

## Procedure

Verify the declared dividend and ex/record/payment dates from primary sources. Calculate payout against earnings and free cash flow using clearly stated definitions. Examine working-capital swings, capex, refinancing, debt covenants, and dilution. Separate recurring operations from asset sales, tax benefits, and other one-offs. Compare issuer with economically similar peers. Present base/downside coverage scenarios and review triggers.

## Expected output

Dividend quality memo with payout, cash coverage, balance-sheet context, one-off adjustments, peer context, and sustainability risks.

## Guardrails

Do not equate dividend yield with return or safety. High yield can reflect price deterioration. Never forecast a distribution without explicit assumptions.

## Evidence standard

For each conclusion, distinguish: (1) supplied observation, (2) deterministic calculation, (3) primary-source fact, (4) interpretation, and (5) unresolved assumption. Preserve source URI, publisher, publication time, observation time, timezone, instrument/venue scope, and data vintage where relevant. If a necessary field is unavailable, mark the result incomplete or abstain; do not infer it from narrative context.
